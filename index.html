Community involvement hours toward your 40-hour graduation requirement

This is your own record, not an official one. Your school makes the final decision on which hours count. Use this to keep track and to fill in your board's Community Involvement form, which still needs a signature from whoever supervised you. Hours generally have to be outside your scheduled class time, and ordinary in-school club meetings usually don't count — so log the events, outreach and volunteering you actually do, not attendance at our meetings.

Teacher feedback form

After we present in a class, that class’s teacher fills in a short Google Form. Paste the link to the form’s response spreadsheet and every answer appears on this page automatically — ratings, student counts and quotes, with nothing copied across by hand.

First time? Four steps.
  1. At forms.new, create a form and add the eight questions below.
  2. In the form’s Settings → Responses, set Collect email addresses to Do not collect, and turn off Restrict to users in your school and Limit to 1 response. Both of those force a Google sign-in, which records who answered.
  3. In the form’s Responses tab, click the Sheets icon → Create a new spreadsheet.
  4. In that spreadsheet: Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer, then copy its link into the box above.

The spreadsheet is readable by anyone holding its link. The form never asks for an email, and asks for a name in one optional question that tells the teacher plainly their name may be quoted on this site, in the year-end report, and in members’ applications. Leaving it blank still submits the feedback.
